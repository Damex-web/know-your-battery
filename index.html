HELP & FAQ

How Know Your Battery works

Answers about the current tools, their assumptions, and features still being planned.

What is Know Your Battery?

A small engineering toolkit for exploring nominal battery-pack calculations and an illustrative 3D pack layout. Other tools shown as “Coming next” are placeholders for planned features.

How do series and parallel connections work?

In a series string, each cell’s positive terminal connects to the next cell’s negative terminal. Series cell voltages add; the string capacity in amp-hours remains the capacity of one cell. Parallel groups connect like polarities together (positive to positive and negative to negative); parallel capacity adds while nominal voltage stays the same. Real pack wiring must follow the cell and protection-system specifications.

What is the difference between voltage, capacity, and energy?

Voltage is electrical potential, measured here in volts (V). Capacity is charge storage, shown in amp-hours (Ah). Energy is the product of nominal voltage and capacity, shown in watt-hours (Wh). The calculator uses Wh = nominal pack voltage × pack capacity.

How are runtime estimates calculated?

This version does not calculate runtime. Runtime depends on usable capacity, load profile, voltage behavior, efficiency, temperature, age, and other factors; a simple capacity-divided-by-current result would only be a rough estimate under stated assumptions.

What does “Cannot verify” mean?

The current app does not provide a general automated “Cannot verify” status. It also does not check user-entered specifications against manufacturer sources. Treat missing or unconfirmed information as unknown; do not assume a value has been verified.

Can I save and export my battery projects?

You can save up to five recent calculations in this browser. They are stored on this device and remain after a page refresh, but they are not synced across browsers or devices. Clearing this site’s browser data may remove them. The My Projects & Reports screen is marked “Coming next”; project saving and export are not currently available.

Does a calculated battery design guarantee safety?

No. These results are nominal estimates and the 3D model is illustrative. A calculation does not certify a pack as safe or ready to build. Review manufacturer specifications, protection requirements, applicable standards, and the complete design with a qualified engineer where needed.

Which battery specifications do I need before starting?

For the current calculator, use a cell’s nominal voltage, rated capacity, chemistry reference, and the intended series and parallel counts. The 3D builder also accepts load current for a nominal power estimate. Check datasheets for operating voltage limits, current limits, charging requirements, temperature limits, and protection guidance before real-world use.

ENGINEERING LIMITATIONS

Engineering Disclaimer

Read the limits of the estimates before applying them to a real battery design.

Know Your Battery is an educational calculation and visualization tool. Its outputs are estimates and are not a certification, safety assessment, or build approval.

Inputs and estimates

Calculations depend on the accuracy and completeness of the values entered. Runtime, charge-time, energy, power, and other estimates can differ from real-world results as cells, loads, temperatures, efficiencies, and operating conditions vary. Runtime and charge-time calculators are not implemented in the current version.

Specifications and missing information

Respect each battery chemistry’s operating limits and the manufacturer’s specifications for voltage, current, charging, temperature, and protection. The app does not validate inputs against datasheets. If data is missing or cannot be confirmed, that check cannot be verified; do not treat an unknown value as a verified specification.

Not a safety certification

A successful calculation does not certify that a battery pack is safe, compatible, or ready to build. The 3D pack view is illustrative and its wires are not a validated physical wiring plan.

Review before building

Real-world designs may require review by a qualified engineer, suitable cell monitoring and protection systems, correctly rated components, and compliance with applicable standards and regulations. Check authoritative manufacturer documentation and obtain appropriate review for the intended application.
